Flagship tool
benchsiDE
benchside.org ↗

Differential expression and cross-dataset analysis of bulk RNA-seq counts, with no R and no install. Filtering, TMM normalization, limma moderated-t and voom with covariates and factorial designs, gene-set and co-expression tests, random-effects meta-analysis across datasets, and replication of a signature in independent data, including across species by homology.

limma · voom covariates & factorial designs meta-analysis cross-species replication validated vs edgeR / limma

Cloning & protein engineering

  • /construct: plasmid construct builder

    Assemble a synthesis-ready coding sequence: fusion and affinity tags, fluorescent proteins and self-labeling tags, linkers, codon optimization for mammalian or E. coli expression, Kozak or Shine-Dalgarno signals, restriction-site avoidance, a cited methods paragraph, and annotated GenBank export.

  • /mutate: site-directed mutagenesis primers

    Design primers for substitutions, insertions, and deletions, with nearest-neighbor Tm, host-optimized codons, overlapping or back-to-back styles, and a ready-to-order table with methods text.

  • /scan: scanning mutagenesis designer

    Alanine, glycine, or saturation scans across a range of positions: per-position primers, degenerate codons, and library complexity, as single-site variants.

  • /degenerate: degenerate codon designer

    Find the minimal IUPAC degenerate codon that encodes a chosen amino-acid set, decode a degenerate codon, and compute library diversity.

Sequence utilities

  • /dna: DNA sequence toolbox

    Clean up pasted sequences, reverse complement, DNA ↔ RNA, change case, rewrap lines, and add or strip position numbers. FASTA-aware.

Writing & publishing

  • /credit: CRediT statement generator

    Paste an author list, check contributor roles in a grid, and copy a journal-ready CRediT author contribution statement.

Built and maintained by the Yaron lab. The utilities are single self-contained pages you can save and run offline; benchsiDE lives at benchside.org.
