Flagship tool
benchsiDE
benchside.org ↗

Differential expression and cross-dataset analysis of bulk RNA-seq counts, with no R and no install. Filtering, TMM normalization, limma moderated-t and voom with covariates and factorial designs, gene-set and co-expression tests, random-effects meta-analysis across datasets, and replication of a signature in independent data, including across species by homology.

limma · voom covariates & factorial designs meta-analysis cross-species replication validated vs edgeR / limma

Utilities

  • /construct: plasmid construct builder

    Assemble a synthesis-ready coding sequence: terminal fusions and affinity tags, fluorescent proteins and self-labeling tags, linkers, codon optimization for mammalian or E. coli expression, Kozak or Shine-Dalgarno signals, and restriction-site avoidance. Generates a methods paragraph with citations and exports annotated GenBank.

  • /dna: DNA sequence toolbox

    Clean up pasted sequences, reverse complement, DNA ↔ RNA, change case, rewrap lines, and add or strip position numbers. FASTA-aware.

  • /credit: CRediT statement generator

    Paste an author list, check contributor roles in a grid, and copy a journal-ready CRediT author contribution statement.
Built and maintained by the Yaron lab. The utilities are single self-contained pages you can save and run offline; benchsiDE lives at benchside.org.
