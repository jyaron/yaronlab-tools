yaronlab.tools/authors

Author & affiliation block builder

Build a title-page author list with automatically numbered affiliations, corresponding-author and equal-contribution marks, and ORCID iDs.

1Authors

Add authors in order. Give each one or more affiliations exactly as they should appear; identical affiliations are merged and numbered automatically. Mark the corresponding author(s) and add ORCID iDs if wanted.

2Options

Part of yaronlab.tools, a set of research utilities from the Yaron lab.
