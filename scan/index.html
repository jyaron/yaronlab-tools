2Scan definition

Alanine and glycine scans skip positions that are already that residue. Saturation randomizes each position independently as single-site variants, one position per clone.

3Primer options

Melting temperatures use the SantaLucia 1998 nearest-neighbor model with the primer in excess (as in IDT, NEB, and Primer3). Set the oligo concentration to match your Tm tool (IDT defaults to 250 nM, NEB protocols to 500 nM).
Part of yaronlab.tools. Shared sequence logic (genetic code, codon usage, nearest-neighbor Tm, degenerate codons) is bundled in the page. Check every primer and library design against your own records before ordering.
