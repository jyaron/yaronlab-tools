yaronlab.tools/wordcount

Manuscript word counter

Live word, character, sentence, and paragraph counts, with options to exclude references, captions, and citations, and a check against common journal limits.

1Manuscript text

Paste a section or the whole manuscript. Counts update live. Use the section selector to count the abstract or body separately, and exclude parts that most journals do not count.

Part of yaronlab.tools, a set of research utilities from the Yaron lab.
