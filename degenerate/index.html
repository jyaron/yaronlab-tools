yaronlab.tools/degenerate

Degenerate codon designer

Find the minimal IUPAC degenerate codon that encodes a chosen set of amino acids, decode a degenerate codon, and compute library diversity.

1Design a degenerate codon

Choose the amino acids you want encoded at a position. The solver returns the IUPAC degenerate codons that cover them with the fewest off-target residues and the least redundancy.

Quick picks:

2Decode a degenerate codon

Enter an IUPAC codon (for example NNK, DTT, GAN) to see which amino acids and codons it specifies.

3Library size

Enter one degenerate codon per randomized position (comma or space separated) to get the theoretical diversity.

Part of yaronlab.tools. Shared sequence logic (genetic code, codon usage, nearest-neighbor Tm, degenerate codons) is bundled in the page. Check every primer and library design against your own records before ordering.
